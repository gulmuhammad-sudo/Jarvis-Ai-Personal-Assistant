HOW TO SWITCH

Press Use on a provider above, or say "Groq pe chalo" / "use OpenRouter" The one you pick answers first and the others stay as backup. "Automatic" goes back to the normal order. Live voice calls always use Gemini Live; switch to Classic style in Settings to talk to another AI by voice.
Add a free cloud AI: Settings › Run setup › AI keys Gemini, Groq or OpenRouter keys are free. They are stored encrypted for your Windows account.
