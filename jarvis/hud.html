CONNECT GMAIL

The app can read your Gmail and save drafts. It can't send, delete or mark anything: that is refused in its code.

  1. Turn on 2-Step Verification in your Google account, if it isn't on.
  2. Make an app password for "Personal Assistant"
  3. Type your Gmail address and those 16 letters here. Never your normal Google password.
Kept encrypted for your Windows account on this PC, and used only with imap.gmail.com. You can take it back any time at myaccount.google.com/apppasswords.
Opening an email here doesn't mark it as read and loads no pictures. The AI sees email only when you ask it to, and asks first (Settings › Permissions).

HOW TO SWITCH

Press Use on a provider above, or say "Groq pe chalo" / "use OpenRouter" The one you pick answers first and the others stay as backup. "Automatic" goes back to the normal order. Live voice calls always use Gemini Live; switch to Classic style in Settings to talk to another AI by voice.
Add a free cloud AI: Settings › Run setup › AI keys Gemini, Groq or OpenRouter keys are free. They are stored encrypted for your Windows account.
